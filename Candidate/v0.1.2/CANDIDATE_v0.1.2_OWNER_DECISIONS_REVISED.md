## D-01 — Provisional Assessment Status semantics

**Issue**  
The Trust Matrix and Functional Specification describe different runtime implications for a Primary source with Provisional Assessment Status. Trust Matrix §5.6 says Primary sources are eligible for primary Skill guidance subject to Provisional status being resolved through formal validation. Functional Specification §4.2 says a Primary source with Provisional status is selected with a light provenance note. The governing interpretation for selection and evidence use remains unresolved.

**Relevant sources**

- [RPA Knowledge Source Trust Matrix v0.1](../../Word/v%200.1.0/RPA_Knowledge_Source_Trust_Matrix_v0.1.docx), §§3.4–3.5, §5.6, and Table 3.4.
- [RPA Development Skill Functional Specification v0.1 Tranche 1](../../Word/v%200.1.0/RPA_Development_Skill_Functional_Specification_v0.1_Tranche1.docx), §§4.1–4.2 and Table 4.1.
- [Candidate v0.1.2](./CAPE_RPA_Development_Skill_CANDIDATE_v0.1.2.md), §§4 and 7.

**Why editorial correction is insufficient**  
The difference concerns the governing meaning of an approved Trust Matrix status and the behavioral eligibility rule, not wording alone. Choosing either interpretation would resolve a governance/requirements question.

**Confirmation required**  
The owner or governance authority must confirm whether Provisional Primary sources may be selected as evidence before formal validation is complete, what caveat or restriction applies, and which artifact controls if the Trust Matrix and Functional Specification remain inconsistent. Any resulting governance change must be recorded through its applicable controlled process.
