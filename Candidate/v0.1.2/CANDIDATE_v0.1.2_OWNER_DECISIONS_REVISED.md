## D-01 — Provisional Assessment Status semantics and authority

**Issue**

The governing artifacts do not state a single, unambiguous runtime interpretation for a source whose Assessment Status is Provisional:

- Trust Matrix v0.1 §5.6 conditions use on formal validation / resolution of Provisional status.
- Functional Specification v0.1 Tranche 1 §4.2 describes selection with a light provenance note.
- Pilot Specification v0.1 §5.6 states that a Provisional source retains full Permitted Use confidence and that the flag is a light caveat, not a reason to downgrade or avoid the source.

The Pilot Specification is marked Draft for Review. Its §5.6 therefore cannot be assumed, without owner confirmation, to supersede or formally interpret the Trust Matrix or Functional Specification. These statements are materially different in their implications for eligibility and use.

**Relevant sources**

- RPA Knowledge Source Trust Matrix v0.1, §§3.4–3.5 and 5.6, and applicable source assessment tables.
- RPA Development Skill Functional Specification v0.1 Tranche 1, §§4.1–4.2 and Table 4.1.
- RPA Development Skill Pilot Specification v0.1, status Draft for Review, §5.6.
- Candidate v0.1.2, §§4 and 7.

**Confirmation required**

The owner / governance authority must confirm: (1) whether Provisional sources may be selected as evidence before formal validation is complete; (2) whether Provisional status changes eligibility or only requires a provenance caveat; (3) which artifact controls if the statements remain inconsistent; and (4) whether the Pilot Specification §5.6 is approved pilot behavior or remains a proposal. Any governance change must follow the applicable controlled process.
